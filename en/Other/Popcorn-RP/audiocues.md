## Resource Details {.tabset}

### Overview

Visual notifications for in-game audio events, designed for players who may not be able to hear the sounds. The README lists event distance and direction, cooldowns, notification duration, timestamp visibility, and custom messages for each event type.

## Commands

Players toggle audio cues with `/audiocues [position]`. The positions are `top`, `left`, `right`, and `bottom`. Position and enabled state are saved with KVP and persist across sessions.
