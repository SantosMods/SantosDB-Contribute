## Resource Details {.tabset}

### Overview

A collection of templates for adding new weapons in FiveM. The README gives these steps: choose the weapon, then replace the placeholders `NEW_ORDER_NUMBER_HERE` (unique), `WEAPON_IN_GAME_NAME_HERE` (unique), `SLOT_NAME_HERE`, and `WEAPON_MODEL_NAME_HERE`.
