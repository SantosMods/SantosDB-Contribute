## Resource Details {.tabset}

### Overview

A Lua prop list for use in FiveM scripts. The author filtered about 19,000 props down to more than 5,000 and sorted them into categories such as barriers and fences, road signs, traffic control, exterior, lighting, nature and vegetation, flags, farm and rural, construction, scaffolding, and equipment and tools.
