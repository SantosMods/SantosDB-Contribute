## Resource Details {.tabset}

### Overview

An upgraded Pipedown Cigarshop MLO in Mirror Park. The README says it was made by EP based on work by Luky00010, that the original MLO shared by Joe is available at `JoeSzymkowiczFiveM/js5m_pipedowncigars`, and that consent to share and rework was given by the original creators.

### Features

* MLO relocated from Mission Row to Mirror Park and scaled to fit
* Optimized custom asset mesh and textures
* Reworked lighting and light shafts on the front windows
* Added backdoor
* Reworked propping
* Audio occlusions
