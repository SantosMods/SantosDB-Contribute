## Resource Details {.tabset}

### Overview

A small clinic for the Grapeseed area, created for Popcorn Roleplay and released to the community. The README states that all files are unlocked.

### Features

* Outside and indoor waiting area
* Parking lot
* Private examination area
* Front desk
* Bathroom
