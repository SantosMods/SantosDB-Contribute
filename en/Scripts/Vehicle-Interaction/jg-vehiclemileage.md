## Resource Details {.tabset}

### Overview

`jg-vehiclemileage` shows an odometer in the bottom right of the screen when a player drives an owned vehicle (stored in the database). The README states it can be configured for miles or kilometers.

A guide for adapting it to a custom framework is in the official documentation.

### Requirements

* QBCore, Qbox, or ESX (or a custom framework)
* `ox_lib`
* `oxmysql`
