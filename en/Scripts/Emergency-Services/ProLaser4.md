## Resource Details {.tabset}

### Overview

`ProLaser4` is a lidar resource for FiveM. It recreates the ProLaser 4 rear display on screen.

### Requirements

* `oxmysql` (optional, for persistent records and the record management tablet)

### Features

* Reticle and HUD for first-person aim down sight
* History and recalled events menu for previous clocks, persistent across relogs
* On-screen recreation of the ProLaser4 rear display
* Audible feedback
