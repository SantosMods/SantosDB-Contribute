## Resource Details {.tabset}

### Overview

`GiveUniformScript` (titled "KA1N3D GiveUniformScript" in the README) lets a player send their uniform details to another player. The recipient can accept and apply the uniform. It supports clothing and props.

### Features

* Share your EUP uniform with another player
* Recipient accepts or denies with a command
* Clothing and prop sharing

## Commands

| Command                 | Purpose                          |
| ----------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| `/giveUniform [Player ID]` | Send your uniform to a player |
| `/acceptUniform`        | Accept a received uniform        |
| `/denyUniform`          | Deny a received uniform          |
